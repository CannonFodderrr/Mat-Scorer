Install MatScorer on iPhone / iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari (required on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with ↑) at the bottom (iPhone) or top (iPad).
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open MatScorer from the Home Screen icon — the address bar stays hidden.

Apple does not allow apps to install with one tap like Android. This is the official iOS method.
